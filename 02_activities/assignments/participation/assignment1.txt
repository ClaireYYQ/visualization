20260603 Class Note

Three important qualities of data visualization:
Is the visualization pleasing to look at? → Aesthetic
Does the visualization accurately and honestly present data? → Substantive
Can we understand what message the maker of the visualization is attempting to
convey? → Perceptual

Effective visualization depends on intented purpose, audience, and medium.

20260610 Class Note

‘Flattening the Curve’ Activity: In what ways is this data visualization inaccessible, and to whom?
1. There is no quantification or tick marks on the x-axis; whether this level of detail is necessary may depend on the target audience.
2. Highlighting in red may not be accessible to individuals with red color vision deficiency.
3. The grey shading of the peak is too subtle, making it difficult to distinguish differences.
4. The text is not accessible to screen readers.

So how do we incorporate these accessibility goals into our data visualization
practices? - Mark size, colors, brightness and contrast, text (including labels, legends, font, typeface, spacing)

To ensure that our data visualizations are accessible to people using a screen reader
or unable to see the image, we include alternate text (alt-text)

Accessibility: Cross-browser functionality, Cost (Open Access or Creative Commons licenses)
